# SDK-011 Functional Requirements

## 02.02 Knowledge Processing

### 1. Purpose

Knowledge processing converts an accepted ingestion item into validated, normalized, provenance-preserving artifacts that downstream chunking, embedding, indexing, retrieval, and context assembly can consume.

### 2. Scope

The processing pipeline covers content acquisition, format verification, safety gates, parsing, normalization, structural extraction, metadata enrichment, language detection, classification hooks, quality validation, artifact persistence, and downstream dispatch.

Chunking, embedding, and indexing algorithms are specified separately.

### 3. Processing Principles

- Source content is immutable; derived artifacts are versioned.
- Every artifact retains source, item, source-version, ingestion-job, processing-job, policy-version, parser-version, and transformation provenance.
- Stages are provider-neutral in domain/application layers.
- Durable stage boundaries support safe retry and replay.
- Raw content is never logged.
- The release is single-deployment; no tenant behavior is required.

### 4. Pipeline State Model

A processing job shall support `PENDING`, `ACQUIRING`, `SCANNING`, `PARSING`, `NORMALIZING`, `ENRICHING`, `VALIDATING`, `PERSISTING`, `DISPATCHING`, `SUCCEEDED`, `SUCCEEDED_WITH_WARNINGS`, `FAILED_RETRYABLE`, `FAILED_TERMINAL`, `QUARANTINED`, `CANCELLING`, and `CANCELLED`.

Only the versioned state machine may change state. Every transition records time, attempt, stage, reason code, correlation ID, and actor/service identity.

### 5. Functional Requirements

#### 5.1 Job creation and orchestration

- **FR-PROC-001:** An accepted ingestion job shall create or resolve one durable processing job for the selected policy and source version.
- **FR-PROC-002:** Repeated dispatch of the same ingestion job and policy version shall not create duplicate logical processing jobs.
- **FR-PROC-003:** A replay under a different policy or parser version shall create a new processing attempt linked to the original history.
- **FR-PROC-004:** The pipeline shall execute only stages declared by the versioned processing policy.
- **FR-PROC-005:** Stage order and dependencies shall be validated before execution.
- **FR-PROC-006:** Jobs shall resume from the last valid durable boundary after restart.
- **FR-PROC-007:** Cancellation shall be checked between stages and during long-running adapters where supported.
- **FR-PROC-008:** Retry policy shall use SDK-004 retry classification and bounded backoff.

#### 5.2 Content acquisition

- **FR-PROC-009:** Inline content or approved content references shall be resolved through a content-access port.
- **FR-PROC-010:** Acquisition shall enforce configured byte limits and timeouts.
- **FR-PROC-011:** Retrieved content length and cryptographic hash shall be verified against supplied values when present.
- **FR-PROC-012:** Redirects, network destinations, filesystem paths, and object locations shall be restricted by approved policies.
- **FR-PROC-013:** Temporary acquisition artifacts shall be encrypted or protected according to classification and deleted according to lifecycle policy.
- **FR-PROC-014:** Acquisition failures shall distinguish unavailable, unauthorized, not found, integrity failure, timeout, throttling, and policy rejection.

#### 5.3 Format detection and safety gates

- **FR-PROC-015:** Declared media type, filename extension, and detected signature shall be compared.
- **FR-PROC-016:** Material type mismatches shall be rejected or quarantined according to policy.
- **FR-PROC-017:** Active content, archives, macros, embedded objects, or executable payloads shall be subject to configured safety policy.
- **FR-PROC-018:** Required malware/safety scanning shall complete successfully before parsing.
- **FR-PROC-019:** Archive expansion shall enforce recursion, file-count, compression-ratio, and total-size limits.
- **FR-PROC-020:** Password-protected or encrypted documents shall be rejected, quarantined, or routed to an approved decryption workflow; credentials shall never be embedded in the job.

#### 5.4 Parser selection and execution

- **FR-PROC-021:** Parsers shall implement a provider-neutral parser SPI.
- **FR-PROC-022:** Parser selection shall be deterministic from detected type, policy, parser availability, and configured priority.
- **FR-PROC-023:** Every parser shall declare supported media types, limits, capabilities, and semantic version.
- **FR-PROC-024:** Parser-specific exceptions shall map to stable SDK-004 errors.
- **FR-PROC-025:** Parser execution shall enforce time, memory, and output-size limits where the runtime supports them.
- **FR-PROC-026:** Parsed output shall preserve ordered text plus structural elements such as pages, sections, headings, paragraphs, tables, lists, captions, links, and code blocks where available.
- **FR-PROC-027:** Unsupported constructs shall produce warnings rather than silent loss when partial output remains useful.
- **FR-PROC-028:** Parser adapters shall pass a shared conformance suite.

#### 5.5 Normalization

- **FR-PROC-029:** Text shall be normalized to a configured Unicode form.
- **FR-PROC-030:** Line endings, control characters, null bytes, and invalid sequences shall be handled deterministically.
- **FR-PROC-031:** Whitespace normalization shall preserve meaningful structural boundaries.
- **FR-PROC-032:** Repeated headers, footers, page numbers, and boilerplate may be removed only by versioned, testable rules.
- **FR-PROC-033:** Normalization shall not translate, summarize, or semantically rewrite source text.
- **FR-PROC-034:** Original offsets or a reversible mapping shall be retained when required for citations and provenance.
- **FR-PROC-035:** Normalization rules and versions shall be attached to derived artifacts.

#### 5.6 Structural extraction

- **FR-PROC-036:** The processor shall produce a canonical document tree or equivalent ordered structure.
- **FR-PROC-037:** Every node shall have stable identity within the artifact version, type, order, content span, and parent relationship.
- **FR-PROC-038:** Page and location references shall be retained where the source format provides them.
- **FR-PROC-039:** Table extraction shall preserve row/column relationships or clearly mark flattened fallback output.
- **FR-PROC-040:** Link targets and referenced media shall be represented safely without automatic unrestricted retrieval.
- **FR-PROC-041:** Empty, duplicate, or structurally corrupt nodes shall be detected and reported.

#### 5.7 Metadata enrichment and classification hooks

- **FR-PROC-042:** Source metadata and derived metadata shall remain distinguishable by provenance.
- **FR-PROC-043:** Language detection shall return language, confidence, detector version, and fallback status.
- **FR-PROC-044:** Title, author, dates, headings, keywords, and document properties may be derived by versioned enrichers.
- **FR-PROC-045:** Enrichers shall use a provider-neutral SPI and declare deterministic/non-deterministic behavior.
- **FR-PROC-046:** Model-assisted enrichment shall record model/provider abstraction identity, prompt/version reference, parameters, and confidence without storing secrets.
- **FR-PROC-047:** Sensitive-data detection and redaction hooks shall use SDK-008 policies and shall never silently alter the immutable source.
- **FR-PROC-048:** Item-level principal/group restrictions shall be propagated to every derived artifact.

#### 5.8 Quality validation

- **FR-PROC-049:** Quality checks shall cover empty output, extraction ratio, invalid-character ratio, structural consistency, language confidence, duplicate content, and configured minimum content.
- **FR-PROC-050:** Quality thresholds shall be versioned by processing policy.
- **FR-PROC-051:** Results shall be `PASS`, `PASS_WITH_WARNINGS`, `QUARANTINE`, or `FAIL` with stable reason codes.
- **FR-PROC-052:** Warnings shall be retained and exposed without leaking raw content.
- **FR-PROC-053:** A failed quality gate shall prevent downstream chunking unless policy explicitly permits degraded processing.

#### 5.9 Artifact persistence and dispatch

- **FR-PROC-054:** Canonical processed artifacts shall be immutable and content-addressable or otherwise integrity-verifiable.
- **FR-PROC-055:** Persistence shall record full provenance and processing-policy versions.
- **FR-PROC-056:** Artifact promotion to current shall use optimistic concurrency and only occur after successful validation.
- **FR-PROC-057:** Superseded artifacts shall remain linked to version history according to retention policy.
- **FR-PROC-058:** Successful persistence and downstream dispatch shall use an outbox or equivalent reliable-publication pattern.
- **FR-PROC-059:** Downstream messages shall reference artifacts rather than embedding large or sensitive content.
- **FR-PROC-060:** Delete and deactivation lifecycle operations shall prevent stale processing completion from restoring retrievability.

#### 5.10 Observability and operations

- **FR-PROC-061:** Metrics shall include stage throughput, duration, queue delay, parser distribution, bytes processed, warnings, retries, quarantine, and failures.
- **FR-PROC-062:** Traces shall span every stage and external adapter call.
- **FR-PROC-063:** Logs shall include safe identities, stage, attempt, result, reason code, correlation, and duration but no raw source text.
- **FR-PROC-064:** Operators shall be able to inspect safe stage history, warnings, and failure categories.
- **FR-PROC-065:** Administrative replay shall identify the selected source version, policy version, parser version, reason, and actor.

### 7. Acceptance Criteria

1. A processing job is durable and idempotently correlated to ingestion, source version, and policy version.
2. Restart resumes from a valid durable boundary without duplicate current artifacts.
3. Content-reference restrictions prevent arbitrary network and filesystem access.
4. Type mismatches, archive bombs, malicious content, integrity failures, and encrypted documents follow deterministic policy outcomes.
5. Parser adapters pass shared conformance tests.
6. Canonical output preserves order, structure, source locations, and provenance where available.
7. Normalization is versioned and does not semantically rewrite content.
8. Quality gates prevent invalid artifacts from reaching chunking.
9. Principal/group access restrictions propagate to every artifact.
10. Stale completion cannot override deletion, deactivation, or a newer current version.
11. Logs, events, traces, and metrics contain no raw content or secrets.
12. Every FR-PROC requirement is linked to test evidence.
