# SDK-011 Functional Requirements

## 02.01 Knowledge Ingestion

### 1. Purpose

This document defines how source content enters the RetailBrainAI Knowledge & Context Framework. Ingestion starts when a source item is submitted, discovered, synchronized, or replayed and ends when a durable canonical job is accepted for downstream processing or reaches a terminal rejected state.

Parsing, chunking, embedding, indexing, retrieval, and context assembly are covered by later documents.

### 2. Release Scope

The current release supports one enterprise deployment. Tenant identifiers and tenant-isolation behavior are not required. The design shall retain neutral extension points—such as optional partition metadata and adapter-level routing—without exposing tenant-specific public contracts.

The ingestion capability shall support:

- source registration and configuration;
- push, pull, batch, event-driven, and administrative ingestion;
- source identity and immutable version history;
- content and metadata validation;
- idempotency and duplicate suppression;
- durable orchestration and status tracking;
- quarantine, retry, replay, cancellation, deletion, and restoration;
- principal/group authorization and source ownership;
- provider-neutral connector contracts;
- structured observability and audit-ready evidence.

### 3. Canonical Model

#### 3.1 KnowledgeSource

A registered origin of knowledge. Required fields are `sourceId`, `name`, `connectorType`, `businessOwner`, `technicalOwner`, `configurationRef`, `classification`, `synchronizationMode`, `processingPolicyRef`, `state`, `createdAt`, and `updatedAt`.

#### 3.2 SourceItem

A uniquely addressable item within a source, identified by `sourceId` and `sourceItemId`.

#### 3.3 SourceVersion

An immutable source-provided or framework-derived revision such as an ETag, object version, commit SHA, row version, timestamp-plus-hash, or domain revision.

#### 3.4 IngestionJob

A durable execution record for `CREATE_OR_UPDATE`, `REPROCESS`, `DEACTIVATE`, `DELETE`, or `RESTORE`.

#### 3.5 CanonicalIngestionEnvelope

The provider-neutral command containing source identity, item identity, operation, version, content or content reference, metadata, security attributes, trace context, and idempotency data.

### 4. Functional Requirements

Requirement identifiers are stable and must appear in implementation plans, automated tests, and acceptance evidence.

#### 4.1 Source management

- **FR-ING-001:** Authorized callers shall register sources with globally unique framework identifiers.
- **FR-ING-002:** Every source shall identify business and technical owners.
- **FR-ING-003:** Connector types shall come from a registered catalogue; unknown or disabled types shall be rejected.
- **FR-ING-004:** Secrets shall be represented only by approved secret references and shall never be returned by normal read APIs.
- **FR-ING-005:** Sources shall declare supported modes: push, pull, batch, event-driven, or administrative.
- **FR-ING-006:** Source states shall be `DRAFT`, `ACTIVE`, `PAUSED`, `DISABLED`, and `DECOMMISSIONED`.
- **FR-ING-007:** Each source shall reference a versioned processing policy.
- **FR-ING-008:** Activation shall require configuration validation and a connector health check where supported.
- **FR-ING-009:** Configuration changes shall be versioned and shall not rewrite prior item history.
- **FR-ING-010:** Pausing a source shall stop new scheduled discovery while preserving accepted jobs and history.

#### 4.2 Request acceptance

- **FR-ING-011:** Every entry point shall map input into one canonical envelope before validation.
- **FR-ING-012:** Required identity fields are `sourceId`, `sourceItemId`, `operation`, and an idempotency key or deterministic derivation inputs.
- **FR-ING-013:** Supported operations are `CREATE_OR_UPDATE`, `REPROCESS`, `DEACTIVATE`, `DELETE`, and `RESTORE`.
- **FR-ING-014:** A request may provide inline content or one approved content reference, but not both.
- **FR-ING-015:** Acceptance shall return job identity, knowledge-item identity, state, timestamp, correlation ID, and duplicate indicator.
- **FR-ING-016:** `ACCEPTED` shall not imply successful downstream processing or retrieval availability.
- **FR-ING-017:** Inline payload and metadata limits shall be enforced before durable acceptance.
- **FR-ING-018:** Unsupported content types shall be rejected or quarantined according to policy.
- **FR-ING-019:** Correlation, causation, and trace identifiers shall be propagated.
- **FR-ING-020:** Authenticated caller, delegated identity when present, and originating application shall be recorded.

#### 4.3 Identity, versioning, and idempotency

- **FR-ING-021:** Logical item identity shall be derived from `sourceId` and `sourceItemId` and remain stable across versions.
- **FR-ING-022:** Source versions shall be captured or deterministically derived.
- **FR-ING-023:** Accessible content shall have a cryptographic hash computed or verified.
- **FR-ING-024:** Repeated equivalent requests with the same idempotency key shall resolve to the original logical job.
- **FR-ING-025:** Reuse of an idempotency key with materially different content shall return a stable conflict error.
- **FR-ING-026:** Duplicate successful source versions shall be a no-op unless explicit reprocessing is requested.
- **FR-ING-027:** Comparable out-of-order versions shall be detected and handled by policy.
- **FR-ING-028:** Conflicting lifecycle operations shall use optimistic concurrency or deterministic serialization.
- **FR-ING-029:** Job, item, source version, policy version, and downstream artifact version relationships shall be retained.

#### 4.4 Validation and security

- **FR-ING-030:** Envelopes and metadata shall be validated against versioned schemas.
- **FR-ING-031:** The source shall exist, be active, and permit the operation.
- **FR-ING-032:** Item identifiers shall conform to connector and platform normalization constraints.
- **FR-ING-033:** Metadata shall be checked for allowed keys, types, sizes, and reserved namespaces.
- **FR-ING-034:** Content references shall use approved schemes and must prevent SSRF, unrestricted network access, and path traversal.
- **FR-ING-035:** Supplied length, hash, signature, or checksum shall be verified where technically possible.
- **FR-ING-036:** Content requiring malware or active-content scanning shall not enter normal processing before a successful result.
- **FR-ING-037:** Classification may be elevated by policy but shall not be lowered without authorization.
- **FR-ING-038:** Items shall carry source-level and item-level principal/group access attributes required by retrieval authorization.
- **FR-ING-039:** Validation errors shall use stable SDK-004 codes and redact secrets and internal paths.

#### 4.5 Orchestration and lifecycle

- **FR-ING-040:** Every accepted request shall create or resolve a durable job before dispatch.
- **FR-ING-041:** States shall include `RECEIVED`, `VALIDATING`, `ACCEPTED`, `QUEUED`, `PROCESSING`, `SUCCEEDED`, `SUCCEEDED_WITH_WARNINGS`, `REJECTED`, `QUARANTINED`, `FAILED_RETRYABLE`, `FAILED_TERMINAL`, `CANCELLING`, and `CANCELLED`.
- **FR-ING-042:** State transitions shall be enforced by a versioned state machine.
- **FR-ING-043:** Accepted-state persistence and work publication shall use an outbox, transactional messaging, or equivalent reliable-dispatch pattern.
- **FR-ING-044:** Jobs shall expose stage, attempt count, last transition, safe progress detail, and terminal summary.
- **FR-ING-045:** Each batch item shall have independent identity and outcome; the batch shall expose aggregate counts.
- **FR-ING-046:** Downstream work shall include canonical identity, content location, metadata, security attributes, policy version, operation, and trace context.
- **FR-ING-047:** Retryable failures shall use bounded exponential backoff with jitter.
- **FR-ING-048:** Retries shall reuse logical operation identity and shall not duplicate versions or artifacts.
- **FR-ING-049:** Authorized operators shall be able to retry, replay, cancel, quarantine, release, reject, deactivate, delete, and restore according to policy.
- **FR-ING-050:** Deletion shall tombstone or remove governed representations and prevent resurrection from delayed events.
- **FR-ING-051:** Legal or compliance holds shall block destructive deletion.

#### 4.6 Pull synchronization

- **FR-ING-052:** Pull connectors shall persist opaque durable checkpoints.
- **FR-ING-053:** At-least-once discovery is allowed when item acceptance remains idempotent.
- **FR-ING-054:** Connectors shall declare full-scan, incremental-scan, or both.
- **FR-ING-055:** Additions, updates, deletions, moves, and permission changes shall map to canonical operations where supported.
- **FR-ING-056:** Missing-item reconciliation shall require confirmation policy rather than deletion after one incomplete scan.
- **FR-ING-057:** Connectors shall honor source rate limits and framework backpressure.
- **FR-ING-058:** Non-partitionable synchronization shall use an exclusive lease or coordinated worker group.

#### 4.7 APIs, events, and observability

- **FR-ING-059:** Authorized callers shall retrieve jobs, item history, source synchronization runs, and bounded filtered job lists.
- **FR-ING-060:** List APIs shall use stable cursor pagination and deterministic ordering.
- **FR-ING-061:** Lifecycle events shall use SDK-005 envelopes and at-least-once delivery semantics.
- **FR-ING-062:** Events shall reference protected content rather than embedding raw sensitive content.
- **FR-ING-063:** Events for one logical item shall include aggregate or lifecycle versions where feasible.
- **FR-ING-064:** Structured logs shall use SDK-003 and exclude raw content, credentials, and unrestricted metadata.
- **FR-ING-065:** Metrics shall cover volume, bytes, latency, queue delay, duplicates, validation failures, retries, quarantine, failures, and synchronization lag.
- **FR-ING-066:** Trace context shall span acceptance, persistence, publication, connector activity, and downstream handoff.
- **FR-ING-067:** Administrative and security-sensitive operations shall produce audit-ready actor, action, target, time, reason, result, and correlation data.

### 6. Acceptance Criteria

Implementation is complete when automated evidence demonstrates that:

1. sources can be registered, validated, activated, paused, and decommissioned;
2. all entry modes map to one canonical envelope;
3. duplicate delivery does not create duplicate logical versions;
4. idempotency conflicts are deterministic;
5. accepted jobs survive restart and cannot be lost between persistence and dispatch;
6. source and principal/group authorization is enforced;
7. content references cannot access arbitrary network or filesystem locations;
8. malicious, unsupported, or ambiguous content is rejected or quarantined;
9. job state history is durable and transition-valid;
10. retry and replay preserve version integrity;
11. permission-only changes propagate to retrieval representations;
12. deletion propagates to derived representations and creates resurrection-safe tombstones;
13. legal holds block destructive deletion;
14. events are versioned and exclude raw sensitive content;
15. logs, traces, and metric labels contain no raw content or secrets;
16. connector checkpoints recover safely after restart;
17. every requirement has automated or explicitly approved manual evidence.
